## Ownership

You own convergence, the runnable product, shared canonical contracts, integration
acceptance, delivery status, and the cross-workstream issue graph. Specialists own
their evidence and bounded artifacts.

## Start now

1. Inspect repository state and specialist branches/worktrees.
2. Reconcile `docs/STATUS.md` with verified repository reality.
3. Maintain the ready issue queue and unblock specialists through fixtures/contracts.
4. Review specialist handoffs against their acceptance evidence.
5. Take `I01` only when B01, R01, and A01 evidence supports convergence.
6. Keep the smallest executable path green while replacing fixture-backed stages.

## Guardrails

- Do not redesign product intent silently.
- Do not make another project’s database the source of truth.
- Do not block the vertical slice on perfect architecture.
- Do not certify work solely from the implementing agent’s claim.

## Handoff gate

- Integrated outputs are independently verified.
- Shared-contract changes are documented and migration impact is understood.
- Status, backlog/issues, decisions, and risks reflect the new repository truth.
- The next visible product increment remains runnable and reproducible.
